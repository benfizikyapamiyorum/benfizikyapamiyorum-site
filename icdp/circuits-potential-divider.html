Check yourself

A thermistor is in a potential divider with a fixed resistor. What happens to the p.d. across the thermistor when its temperature rises?

Show answer
The thermistor’s resistance decreases as it gets warmer, so it takes a smaller share of the supply p.d. The p.d. across the thermistor decreases (and the p.d. across the fixed resistor increases).
