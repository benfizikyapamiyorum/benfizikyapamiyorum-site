Check yourself

1. Core Water waves travel at 0.24 m/s with a frequency of 4.0 Hz. What is their wavelength?

λ = v ÷ f = 0.24 ÷ 4.0 = 0.060 m (6.0 cm).

2. Core The frequency of the paddle is doubled but the water depth is unchanged. What happens?

The speed depends on the depth, not the frequency. With v fixed, doubling f halves λ.

3. Core Water waves pass from deep water into shallow water. Which statement is correct?

Waves travel more slowly in shallower water. The frequency is set by the source and does not change, so λ = v ÷ f gets smaller. If they arrive at an angle they change direction: refraction.

4. Supplement When do waves diffract (spread out) most after passing through a gap?

Diffraction is greatest when the gap width is similar to the wavelength. A gap much wider than the wavelength lets the waves through with only a little spreading at the edges.

5. Core Waves hit a straight barrier with an angle of incidence of 35°. What is the angle of reflection?

For reflection at a plane surface, the angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence: 35°. Both are measured from the normal.
