Check yourself

Why does the skydiver’s acceleration decrease as she speeds up?

Show answer
Air resistance (drag) increases as her speed increases, so the resultant force (weight − drag) gets smaller and so does her acceleration. When drag equals weight the resultant force is zero and she falls at a constant terminal velocity.
