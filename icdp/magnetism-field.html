Magnetic Fields and Electromagnets

Try this: Drag the plotting compass around the field. Press Plot a point again and again: each dot is marked at the north end of the needle and the compass moves on, so the dots trace out a field line, just like in the lab. Then switch to a wire or a solenoid and reverse the current.
Make a prediction first: two magnets are placed with north poles facing. Will there be a point between them where a compass feels no force? Where? Choose Repel (N–N) and use the compass to check.

Field lines go from the north pole to the south pole outside a magnet. The red end of each needle is its north pole. The Earth's magnetic field is ignored.
