Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 · 4.5 Electromagnetic effects

Motors, Generators and Transformers

Try this: Start with the d.c. motor: reverse the current or swap the magnet and watch the direction of rotation. Then push a magnet into a coil in Induction, see the a.c. output of a generator, and change the turns on a transformer.
Make a prediction first: what happens to the galvanometer needle if you hold the magnet still inside the coil? And if you pull it out again? Decide, then try it in Induction.

Controls

You can also drag the magnet with your finger or mouse.

The coil is connected to the circuit through slip rings and brushes, so the output is alternating current.
