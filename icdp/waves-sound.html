Check yourself

1. Core What is the approximate range of frequencies that humans can hear?

The audible range for humans is about 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz (20 kHz). Sound above 20 kHz is ultrasound.

2. Core A student stands 170 m from a wall and hears the echo of a clap 1.0 s later. What is the speed of sound?

Speed = distance ÷ time = (2 × 170 m) ÷ 1.0 s = 340 m/s.

3. Core The amplitude of a sound wave is increased but the frequency stays the same. What do you hear?

A larger amplitude gives a louder sound. The pitch depends on the frequency, which has not changed.

4. Supplement A ship's sonar pulse returns from the sea bed 0.40 s after it is sent. The speed of sound in sea water is 1500 m/s. How deep is the sea?

The pulse travels 1500 × 0.40 = 600 m there and back, so the depth is 600 ÷ 2 = 300 m.

5. Supplement What is a compression in a sound wave?

In a compression the air particles are pushed closer together (higher pressure). In a rarefaction they are further apart (lower pressure).
