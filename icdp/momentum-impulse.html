Check yourself

1. A 60 kg passenger travelling at 20 m/s is brought to rest. What is the size of the change in momentum?

Δp = m Δv = 60 kg × 20 m/s = 1200 kg m/s.

2. The passenger in question 1 is stopped in 0.05 s without an airbag and in 0.40 s with one. What are the average forces?

F = 1200 ÷ 0.05 = 24 000 N without the airbag and F = 1200 ÷ 0.40 = 3000 N with it. Eight times the time gives one-eighth of the force.

3. Why does an airbag reduce injuries?

The change in momentum is fixed by the mass and speed. The airbag increases the time taken to stop, so F = Δp ÷ Δt is smaller.

4. What does the area under a force–time graph represent?

Area = force × time = impulse, which equals the change in momentum, Δ(m v).

5. A racket exerts an average force of 200 N on a ball for 0.010 s. What impulse does the ball receive?

Impulse = F Δt = 200 N × 0.010 s = 2.0 N s, so the ball's momentum changes by 2.0 kg m/s.
