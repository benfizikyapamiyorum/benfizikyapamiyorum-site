I Can't Do Physics

Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625

Interactive simulations

28 simulations covering all six topics of the syllabus. Drag, slide and experiment. Each simulation goes with a worksheet in our IGCSE Physics packs and works on phones, tablets and computers.

Topic 1.2

Motion

Distance–Time and Speed–Time GraphsDrive a car through three stages and watch both motion graphs draw themselves.Free Fall and Terminal VelocityDrop a skydiver with and without air resistance, then open the parachute.

Topics 1.1 · 1.3 · 1.4

Mass, Weight & Density

Density by DisplacementWeigh an irregular object, lower it into a measuring cylinder and calculate its density.Float or Sink?Change the density of a block and the liquid it is in. Does it float or sink?

Topic 1.5

Forces

Stretching a SpringAdd masses to a spring, measure the extension and find the limit of proportionality.Balance the Beam: MomentsDrag weights onto a pivoted beam and balance the clockwise and anticlockwise moments.

Topic 1.6

Momentum

Collisions and Conservation of MomentumCrash two trolleys together with bouncy or sticky buffers and check that the total momentum stays the same.Crumple Zones, Airbags and ImpulseStop a car passenger in different times: same change in momentum, very different force.

Topic 1.7

Energy, Work & Power

Roller Coaster EnergyWatch gravitational potential energy turn into kinetic energy, with or without friction.Efficiency and Sankey DiagramsBuild a to-scale Sankey diagram for different devices and calculate efficiency.

Topics 1.8 · 2.1.3

Pressure

Pressure in LiquidsMove pressure gauges through different liquids, then see how a brick’s pressure depends on its area.Boyle’s Law: Squashing a GasPush the plunger of a sealed syringe and plot pressure against volume and against 1/V.

Topics 2.1 · 2.2 · 2.3

Thermal Physics

Heating Curve of WaterHeat ice and watch the temperature–time graph: rising sections, flat sections and specific heat capacity.Particles, Evaporation and Heat TransferParticles in solids, liquids and gases, why evaporation cools, and conduction, convection and radiation.

Topics 3.1 · 3.4

Waves & Sound

Ripple Tank: Reflection, Refraction and DiffractionChange the frequency and depth and watch water waves reflect, refract and diffract through a gap.Sound: Pitch, Loudness and EchoesSee compressions and rarefactions, read an oscilloscope trace and time an echo to find the speed of sound.

Topics 3.2 · 3.3

Light & the Electromagnetic Spectrum

Refraction and Total Internal ReflectionMeasure the angles in a glass block and find the critical angle with a semicircular block.Converging Lens Ray DiagramsMove an object along the axis of a thin converging lens and watch the rays find the image.The Electromagnetic SpectrumFrom radio waves to gamma rays: wavelengths, frequencies, uses, hazards and dispersion by a prism.

Topic 4.2

Electrical Quantities

Current–Voltage GraphsSweep the voltage across a resistor, a filament lamp and a diode and compare their I–V graphs.Resistance of a WireChange the length, thickness and material of a wire and see how its resistance changes.

Topic 4.3

Electric Circuits

Series and Parallel CircuitsBuild series and parallel circuits with two resistors and read the meters.Potential Divider SensorsUse a thermistor or an LDR in a potential divider and watch the output voltage change.

Topics 4.1 · 4.5

Magnetism & Electromagnetism

Magnetic Fields and ElectromagnetsPlot the field of bar magnets, a straight wire and a solenoid with a compass.Motors, Generators and TransformersSpin a d.c. motor, induce a current with a magnet, watch an a.c. generator and design a transformer.

Topic 5.2

Nuclear Physics

Radioactive Decay and Half-LifeWatch unstable nuclei decay at random and find the half-life from the count-rate graph.Alpha, Beta and Gamma RadiationTest what stops each radiation, bend them in electric and magnetic fields and balance decay equations.

Topic 6

Space Physics

Orbits, Seasons and the StarsOrbital speeds, day and night, the Moon’s phases, the seasons and distances in light-years.
