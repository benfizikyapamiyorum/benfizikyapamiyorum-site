Check yourself

A motor is supplied with 500 J of energy and does 350 J of useful work. What is its efficiency and how much energy is wasted?

Show answer
Efficiency = useful energy output ÷ total energy input × 100% = 350 ÷ 500 × 100% = 70%. Wasted energy = 500 − 350 = 150 J.
