Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 · 3.2.2 Refraction of light

Refraction and Total Internal Reflection

Try this: Change the angle of incidence and watch the ray bend towards the normal as it enters the glass. Then switch to the Semicircular block: the ray now leaves the glass, bends away from the normal and, beyond the critical angle, is totally internally reflected.
Make a prediction first: in the semicircular block, what will happen to the ray in the air when the angle of incidence goes past about 42° in glass (n = 1.50)? Decide, then move the slider and check.

Dashed line = normal (at 90° to the surface). Angles are measured from the normal, not from the surface. The faint ray is the weak partial reflection.
