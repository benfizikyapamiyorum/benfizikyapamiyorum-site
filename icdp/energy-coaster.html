Check yourself

A 500 kg car rolls from rest down a 20 m drop with no friction. What is its speed at the bottom? (g = 9.8 N/kg)

Show answer
mgh = ½mv², so v = √(2gh) = √(2 × 9.8 × 20) = 19.8 m/s (about 20 m/s). The mass cancels.
