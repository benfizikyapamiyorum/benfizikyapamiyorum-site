Collisions and Conservation of Momentum

Check yourself

1. A 2.0 kg trolley moves at 3.0 m/s. What is its momentum?

p = m v = 2.0 kg × 3.0 m/s = 6.0 kg m/s.

2. A 1.0 kg trolley at 4.0 m/s hits a 3.0 kg trolley at rest. They stick together. What is their velocity afterwards?

Total momentum before = 1.0 × 4.0 + 3.0 × 0 = 4.0 kg m/s. After: (1.0 + 3.0) kg × v = 4.0 kg m/s, so v = 1.0 m/s.

3. In the sticky collision of question 2, what happens to the total kinetic energy?

Before: ½ × 1.0 × 4.0² = 8 J. After: ½ × 4.0 × 1.0² = 2 J. Momentum is conserved but 6 J of kinetic energy is transferred to internal (thermal) energy and sound.

4. A trolley's momentum changes from +3.0 kg m/s to −1.0 kg m/s during a collision lasting 0.050 s. What is the size of the average force on it?

Δp = −1.0 − (+3.0) = −4.0 kg m/s. F = Δp ÷ Δt = 4.0 ÷ 0.050 = 80 N (directed to the left).

5. Two trolleys move towards each other with momenta of +2.0 kg m/s and −2.0 kg m/s. They stick together. What happens next?

Total momentum = +2.0 + (−2.0) = 0, so after they join the total momentum is still 0 and they stop.
