Density by Displacement

Check yourself

A stone raises the water level from 40 cm³ to 55 cm³. Its mass is 39 g. What is its density?

Show answer
Volume = 55 − 40 = 15 cm³. Density = m ÷ V = 39 g ÷ 15 cm³ = 2.6 g/cm³.
