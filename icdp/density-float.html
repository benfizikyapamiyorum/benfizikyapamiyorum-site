Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 · 1.4 Density

Float or Sink?

Try this: Change the density of the block with the slider and choose a liquid. Drag the block down and let go. An object floats if its density is less than the density of the liquid.

Tip: drag the block up or down and let go.

The block (10 cm × 10 cm × 10 cm = 1000 cm³)

The liquid

density of block0.60 g/cm³
density of liquid1.00 g/cm³
mass of block600 g
fraction below surface60%

When it floats, the fraction of the block below the surface = density of block ÷ density of liquid.

Check yourself

A block has a density of 0.95 g/cm³. Does it float in water (1.00 g/cm³)? Does it float in olive oil (0.92 g/cm³)?

Show answer
It floats in water because 0.95 g/cm³ is less than 1.00 g/cm³. It sinks in olive oil because 0.95 g/cm³ is greater than 0.92 g/cm³.
