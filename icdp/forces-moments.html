Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625 · 1.5.2 Turning effect of forces

Balance the Beam: Moments

Try this: Drag the weights from the tray onto the beam. The beam balances when the total clockwise moment equals the total anticlockwise moment. Drag a weight off the beam to remove it.

Drag a weight onto the beam. It snaps to the nearest 0.05 m. Drag it below the beam to put it back in the tray.

Controls

The beam is uniform and pivoted at its centre, so its own weight has no moment about the pivot. Principle of moments: when the beam is balanced, total clockwise moment = total anticlockwise moment.

Check yourself

A 4 N weight is 0.30 m to the left of the pivot. Where must a 6 N weight go to balance the beam?

Show answer
Anticlockwise moment = 4 N × 0.30 m = 1.2 N m. For balance, 6 N × d = 1.2 N m, so d = 0.20 m to the right of the pivot.
