Wave equation calculator: v = f λ

All electromagnetic waves travel at 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum (and almost the same in air) Supplement, so λ = v ÷ f. Doubling the frequency halves the wavelength.

Dispersion of white light by a prism Core

What is happening?

Each colour has a different frequency and travels at a slightly different speed in glass, so each colour refracts by a different amount. Violet (highest frequency, shortest wavelength) is refracted the most and red (lowest frequency, longest wavelength) the least.

Order of colours: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet
frequency increases →, wavelength decreases →

Light of a single frequency is called monochromatic Supplement. It is refracted but not split up. The spread of colours in the diagram is exaggerated so that you can see it.
