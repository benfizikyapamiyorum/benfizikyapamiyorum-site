Radioactive Decay and Half-Life

Try this: Press Start and watch the nuclei decay one by one, at random. The detector counts every decay plus the background radiation. Read the half-life from the count-rate graph: find the time for the count rate to fall to half its starting value.
Make a prediction first: with a half-life of 6 s, how many of the 1000 nuclei will be left after 12 s? Will the answer be exactly the same every time you run it?
